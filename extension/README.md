# EthioDV Operator Auto-Filler Chrome Extension

**1-Click DOM Auto-Filler for the Official U.S. Diversity Visa Portal (`dvprogram.state.gov`)**

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome (10 Seconds)

1. Open Google Chrome on your computer.
2. In the URL bar, go to: `chrome://extensions/`
3. In the top-right corner, turn **ON** the switch for **Developer mode**.
4. In the top-left corner, click the button: **"Load unpacked"**.
5. Select the `extension` folder located inside your project:
   `c:\Users\Biruh\Desktop\Dvfilltrack\extension`
6. Click **Select Folder**. The **"EthioDV Operator Auto-Filler"** icon will now appear in your Chrome toolbar!

---

## ⚡ How Operators File an Application in 30 Seconds

1. In the **EthioDV Admin Dashboard**, open the **Filing Station**.
2. Click **"Claim Next Entry"** (or resume your active filing).
3. Click the purple button: **⚡ "Copy Extension Payload"**.
   *(Your clipboard now securely contains the complete decrypted JSON payload for the primary entrant, spouse, and children).*
4. In another Chrome tab, open the official portal: `https://dvprogram.state.gov/application.aspx`
5. Click the **EthioDV Auto-Filler** extension icon in your Chrome toolbar.
6. Click **"1. Auto-Fill Part 1 (Primary Entrant)"**.
   *(Every single field—names, birth date, address, phone, email, education, and marital status—is filled into the official form inputs in 1 second!)*
7. Upload the applicant's photo, solve the CAPTCHA, and click **Continue**.
8. If the applicant has a spouse or children, click **"2. Auto-Fill Part 2 (Spouse & Children)"** on the derivative page!
9. Submit and copy the official 16-character Confirmation Number back into the EthioDV Filing Station.
